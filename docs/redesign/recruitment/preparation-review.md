# Starter preparation: review and remaining connections

## Intended result

Each pathway contains 14 original starter lessons at a suggested 180 minutes per day (42 hours in total). A trainee reads a small explanation, makes a concrete artefact, tests an idea, rehearses teaching and reflects. This is induction, not the full school curriculum or a promise of employment. People keep a useful portfolio even if recruitment does not continue.

The seven locally saved reference PDFs are design, Excel, Scrum, Think Python, networking, NIST cloud definitions and the combined Nigerian cybercrime publication. They are validated PDF files with page counts and SHA-256 records in the local library's sources.json. Several original export URLs were blocked; the manifest keeps those failures and identifies successful lawful alternatives. No claim of an exhaustive worldwide book collection is made. Changing software is better supported with official current documentation than with a pile of outdated books.

The supplied copyrighted books and downloaded noncommercial reference works are not copied into public site assets or committed to Git. Only original Forge lesson text, the original Canva workbook and an annotated source catalogue are part of the site. A private background citation is not required trainee homework; the original notes support the starter activity without access to the owner's PDFs.

## Practical review guide

Review an artefact and its explanation, not how confidently someone claims expertise. Look for accuracy; clear demonstration; a learner's independent attempt; respectful feedback; a workable low-device option; privacy and safety; and an honest limitation. Ask for a revision with a specific next action. A formative practice answer never changes recruitment assessment results or grants a role. Do not use the journal download as a completion certificate.
