# Content principles for the Forge redesign

Source: founder's essay “The Future We Keep Waiting For”, the movement brief, and approved page direction. Apply these across every rewritten page.

1. Start with the person and the practical opportunity. Explain why the work matters before listing features.
2. Preserve the founder's reflective, passionate voice: ask useful questions, acknowledge reality and invite responsibility. Avoid generic startup claims and guilt-driven pressure.
3. Explain the technical substance plainly. A programme page must say what learners do, how teaching works and what support makes it possible; a movement page must explain eligibility, matching, devices and the learning commitment.
4. Earn trust with verified examples. Do not invent statistics, testimonials, product features, service coverage or certainty about social outcomes.
5. Give each page one clear next step, with other relevant participation routes available. Visitors should understand what their enquiry starts.
6. Distinguish Forge Intellitech Services Ltd (company) from Forge ITs (movement within it). Endless Possibilities is part of that movement.
7. Design for leads without hiding the detail. Use inviting short copy first, then honest processes and expandable answers.
8. YouTube advertisements should lead to the relevant company/programme page. About Forge, TechUp and ElyTech also need their approved YouTube video links. Founder confirmed on 20 October 2026 that these videos/URLs are not ready; do not add guessed links or empty players.
9. Programme content must include approved skills/teaching approach, internet provision, dashboard integration, Saturday live class and downloadable records/projects as a leaving-school portfolio. Verify specifics before promising scope or availability. Detailed interactive animation follows the page-building phase.
10. Final animation phase: hero, opt-in letter-collecting character, BiteBank bite, Trego bus, TechUp graph growth, Forge All-In rings joining. Support touch/keyboard and reduced motion. No production release until explicit final approval of the completed website.
