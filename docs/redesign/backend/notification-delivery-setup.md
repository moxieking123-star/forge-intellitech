Each queue claims at most two jobs per invocation, leases for five minutes, retries at exponential intervals, and stops after six attempts. Provider idempotency keys are stable per queue/job, including crash recovery. The 23-hour guard deliberately stops ambiguous delivery before Resend's 24-hour key lifetime; old failed jobs require provider-dashboard reconciliation and a deliberate operational decision. `forge_notification_retry(queue,id)` is service-only, refuses sent jobs and expired windows, and must only be exposed through an independently authorized staff action with audit. The /admin/notifications workspace exposes authenticated, audited status and safe retry. Never reset first_attempt_at or change a job's content/recipient after an attempt. Notification bodies use fixed templates and destinations; untrusted content is plain text. Proposal recipients are bound to the durable validated request.

A provider acceptance marks sent; it does not establish inbox delivery. Bounce/webhook handling and automated daily provider quota monitoring are not implemented. Resend free-plan account limits and verified-domain eligibility must be confirmed in the operator's dashboard before activation; this worker does not purchase a plan or fall back to paid SMTP. Configure scheduler frequency to fit those limits.

Rollback: disable EMAIL_DELIVERY_ENABLED first, stop scheduler, retain outbox records for reconciliation. Do not drop tables containing delivery history. Migration rollback should be performed from a database backup or reviewed reverse migration after reconciliation; there is no automatic destructive down migration.
