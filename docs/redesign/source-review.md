## Working voice

The strongest reference is the founder's current brief and original words in the relevant conversations: direct, hopeful, practical, ambitious, with a clear invitation to help. Use familiar words and specific actions. Preserve the conviction that people can start creating solutions within their present capacity. Avoid borrowed corporate language, fear-based claims about children, exaggerated outcomes and unsupported investment promises.
