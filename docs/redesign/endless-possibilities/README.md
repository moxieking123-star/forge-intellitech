The founder explicitly requested use of old-site photographs for this recap, superseding the earlier no-child-imagery preference for these existing event photos. No new generated child imagery is used. Existing JPGs are preserved; optimized WebPs are placed under `public/images/movement/`.

Timing: on 20 October 2026, next year means 2027. LaunchPad 2027 is in planning with the founder's bigger-and-better ambition. No dates, fees, prizes, application window, participant count or venue are invented. Stale 2026 announcements are not reused. The old `/launchpad` route now redirects to the nested annual LaunchPad page; in-school links point to their distinct initiative.

In-school LaunchPad: schools offering TechUp; identify ideas/projects and work alongside students towards enterprise. No standard equity, grant or ownership terms are asserted. Internship: recent SSCE completers connected to schools offering Forge technology education, paid practical experience and education support during the internship. Government-school seminars are a separate creativity/exposure initiative, without the TechUp-school restriction.

The hub explains purpose, youth potential, opportunity and shared responsibility, with six participation choices and links to all initiatives. Dedicated donor/student intake forms remain a subsequent task; existing contact enquiries are explicitly labelled as conversations, not automatic applications or confirmed places. Formal student/parent agreements are deferred as requested. Animations remain for the final whole-site phase. YouTube links are pending from the founder.
