This programme overview introduces ElyTech and TechUp. It is not a confirmed commercial proposal. Delivery arrangements and commercial terms are discussed with each school and shared privately.

ElyTech · P1–P6

A technology subject for all primary pupils, connecting four skills: Chisanbop finger arithmetic, touch typing without looking at the keys, Scratch coding logic and Canva design. P1–P3 prioritise typing and Chisanbop; P3 is the transition into broader P3–P6 practice across all four skills. Forge supplies Chisanbop learning aids and practice keyboards.

TechUp · JSS1–SS3

JSS1–JSS3 build a foundation in Canva/Figma design, organising data with Excel, Power BI, HTML/CSS coding, cyber citizenship and awareness of Nigerian cybersecurity law. AI-assisted content writing, planning and digital marketing also form part of the foundation.

SS1–SS3 choose a pathway: Builder (websites, software, automations, data tools and prototypes), Storyteller (design, writing, video, branding, media and presentations), or Strategist (research, data, business, product thinking, marketing and planning). Saturday live classes support practice and questions.

Prepare for the subject

These are school subjects for all students. Learners use their own gadgets; parents arrange age-appropriate laptops from Basic 3 upward. An internet-capable laptop of any specification can support school classes. Without a classroom device, a learner can later practise guided dashboard activities on a parent’s device when access is activated.

Schools can request supplier introductions for used laptops below NGN100,000, depending on availability. Suppliers may offer instalments under their terms; availability and approval are not guaranteed.

Support around the learning

  • Internet provision: school context and connectivity arrangements are discussed during onboarding.
  • Dashboard: currently in testing pending onboarding activation. Learning records, projects and portfolio downloads are planned; access depends on setup being ready.
  • School communication: once the school completes onboarding, families prepare age-appropriate devices. The school sends portal login details when setup is ready.

How a school begins

Onboarding for next term is open through 31 December 2026.

  1. Share your school name, location, contact and primary or secondary learner groups.
  2. Discuss your learners, existing technology setup and teaching needs with Forge.
  3. Review practical delivery arrangements and the commercial proposal shared privately.
  4. Agree the next steps for onboarding, connectivity, dashboard access and learning arrangements.

Contact the school for its fee and family preparation information. Proposals are shared privately with schools.
