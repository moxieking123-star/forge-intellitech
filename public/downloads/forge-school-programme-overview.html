This programme overview introduces ElyTech and TechUp. It is not a confirmed commercial proposal. Delivery arrangements and commercial terms are discussed with each school and shared privately.

ElyTech · primary schools

Age-appropriate exploration builds digital confidence through guided activities, coding foundations, design and problem-solving. Clear demonstrations and supported practice give younger learners room to ask questions, try things and make simple projects.

TechUp · secondary schools

Practical digital skills, coding foundations and design connect to recognisable problems. Guided instruction leads into project work: breaking a task down, building a first version, testing it, receiving feedback and explaining decisions.

Support around the learning

  • Internet provision: supports digital learning activities. School context and connectivity arrangements are discussed during onboarding.
  • Dashboard integration: connects student learning records and project work with the programme. Access and setup are discussed with the school.
  • Saturday live class: another opportunity for guided learning and questions. Relevant learner groups and joining arrangements are confirmed with the school.
  • A leaving-school portfolio: downloadable student records and projects help learners carry evidence of their exploration and progress forward. Download arrangements are covered in dashboard setup.

How a school begins

  1. Share your school name, location, contact and primary or secondary learner groups.
  2. Discuss your learners, existing technology setup and teaching needs with Forge.
  3. Review practical delivery arrangements and the commercial proposal shared privately.
  4. Agree the next steps for onboarding, connectivity, dashboard access and learning arrangements.

Contact the school for any parent-facing fee or joining information. Device purchases are separate decisions. Programme enrolment does not guarantee a laptop; the Forge ITs laptop campaign has its own eligibility, verification and matching process.
